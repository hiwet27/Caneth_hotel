Our Story

Caneth Hotel is our renown of historic heritage and contemporary philosophy, born from dedication to bespoke service and architectural splendor. Originally a landmark estate from 1923, we've transformed into an urban sanctuary that preserves soul while embracing modern artistry.

At Caneth Hotel, we offer an unparalleled experience that blends comfort, elegance, and personalized service. Nestled in a serene environment, our hotel is designed to provide ultimate relaxation while you explore the beauty of Ethiopia.
